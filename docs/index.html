A map of the machine proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

The proof is 17 million lines of Lean, which no one will read line by line. This site reads the compiled proof terms instead and shows how the proof is put together: which results carry it, which published theorems it formalizes, and where the ideas of the announcement live in the code.

    How this was made

    • The published compiled proof (Lean 4.33.1, commit 40e56bab) was streamed file by file. For every constant, every other constant in its statement or proof term was recorded. This includes what Lean filled in by itself: instances, simp lemmas, automation.
    • Auto-generated helpers (proof_1, match_1, equation lemmas, a structure's fields) are folded into the result they belong to. Results from Mathlib and Lean core are left out.
    • "Owns" comes from a dominator analysis: X owns Y when every chain of uses from the main theorems down to Y passes through X.
    • Plain-English summaries are marked as written by Claude from the Lean statements and names. They are a reading aid; the Lean source is the authority.
    • This shows how the proof is put together. It does not check it; Lean's kernel and independent checkers did that.
